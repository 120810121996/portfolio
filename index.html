Featured Projects

Intelligent Shelf Audit System

Shelf audits at UCB were done manually, a slow, costly process with up to 30% error rate and reports taking 2–3 days to reach managers. We built an end-to-end AI platform for UCB (Union Camerounaise de Brasseries) that lets field agents photograph shelves and instantly detect products and prices via computer vision. Reports are now transmitted in seconds, errors are drastically reduced, and our YOLOv11s model achieved 94% mAP on test data.

Stack: Python · FastAPI · YOLOv11s · PaddleOCR · Flutter · Firebase · React · Plotly.js

Automated IT Tenders & Job Crawler

An intelligent backend automation engine that scrapes, filters, and alerts on IT opportunities. It aggregates data from multiple international and regional organizations. Built with specialized scraping pipelines (Playwright, BeautifulSoup, curl-cffi, APIs) and an advanced rule-based matching engine that filters out noise and detects target African and international locations before sending daily SMTP email reports.

Stack: Python · Playwright · BeautifulSoup · curl-cffi · Feedparser · APIs · SMTP · Regex

Multi-Category Quality Assessment of Safou using CNNs

Developed and evaluated state-of-the-art Deep Learning models (ResNet-50, ReXNet-150, DenseNet-121) for the multi-class quality classification of African Plums (Dacryodes edulis / Safou) to automate post-harvest sorting and reduce food waste. Trained on a dataset of 4,507 annotated images across six quality states (bruised, cracked, spotted, rotten, unripe, and unaffected). Handled class imbalance using WeightedRandomSampler and achieved a top accuracy of 91.07% with ResNet-50.

Stack: Python · PyTorch · ResNet · ReXNet · DenseNet · Jupyter Notebooks · Transfer Learning

E-commerce Customer Behavior Analysis

Comprehensive analysis of e-commerce customer behavior using a dataset of 350 customers with 11 variables. Identified key insights for improving customer experience and marketing strategies through exploratory analysis and linear regression.

Key Findings: Gold customers and San Francisco residents are the biggest buyers, with men spending slightly more than women.

Human Action Recognition

Developed a model for recognizing human actions in videos by comparing 3D CNN and MobileNetV2 with LSTM architectures. The transfer learning approach achieved 95% accuracy, significantly outperforming the 3D CNN model (72%).

Technologies: Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Transfer Learning

Climate Change Impact on Agriculture

Analysis of 10,000 data points revealing crop yield impacts from CO₂ levels and extreme weather events. Random Forest model predicts 20% yield decline risk by 2030 in vulnerable regions.

Recommendations: Crop adaptation, optimized irrigation, and CO₂ reduction strategies.

Tomato Leaf Disease Detection

Automated system for detecting and classifying tomato leaf diseases using YOLOv8 model. Achieved 92% accuracy in identifying 10 distinct disease categories, enabling rapid diagnosis and targeted treatment for farmers.

Impact: Supports sustainable agricultural practices and minimizes crop losses.
